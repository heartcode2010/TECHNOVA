# TECHNOVA complete product scope register

This register is derived from the 17-page TECHNOVA Master Product Vision & Build Specification (v1.0, 21 November 2026). It is the long-term scope map; it does not imply every module is implemented or approved for simultaneous launch.

## Connected user journey

Discover a problem or goal → diagnose → learn → practise → prove skills → match to an opportunity/client → deliver work → transact through supported providers → compound reputation and opportunity.

TECHNOVA One should connect one account, identity, secure wallet/ledger experience, notification centre, search, portfolio and independent specialist services. Users must be able to use individual services without being forced into the entire ecosystem. Privacy settings determine what profile/activity information is public.

## Signature concepts to evaluate

- **Local Problem Bounties:** moderated local problems posted by residents, schools, SMEs and NGOs; sponsor-funded milestones and team solutions.
- **Proof-of-Work Passport:** inspectable evidence from assessed projects, challenge outcomes, verified course completion and completed client work. Attendance alone is not competence.
- **Trust Escrow for Digital Work:** scope, acceptance criteria, payment status, evidence, revisions and dispute workflows through regulated partners; do not unlawfully hold funds.
- **SME Digital Health Check:** non-invasive assessment of website, online presence, backups, payment workflow, customer-data handling and cyber hygiene.
- **Opportunity-to-Application Copilot:** turn a real listing into eligibility self-check, checklist, draft documents, deadline plan and tracker; user approves every submission.
- **Community Signal Network:** opt-in privacy-preserving reports about scams, connectivity, power and service reliability with moderation, timestamps and geographic aggregation.
- **Digital Skills-to-Contract Pathway:** sponsor-funded practical challenge/cohort that can lead to paid internship, contract or interview under transparent rules.
- **One-Click Business Presence:** simple storefront, WhatsApp catalogue, payment instructions, invoice flow and customer record for small traders.

## Monetisation and integrity

Potential revenue: memberships; metered AI credits; course/certification/exam purchases; marketplace commissions; SME/school subscriptions; website monitoring/security services; API plans; sponsor-funded competitions/cohorts; optional affiliate programme with transparent configurable terms.

Keep pending earnings, payable balances, ledger balances and actual provider-held funds distinct. Use signed webhooks, idempotency, reconciliation, audit logs and maker-checker payout approval. A screenshot is not proof of payment. Validate NGN pricing with Nigerian users; do not assume a payment or escrow provider is available or compliant.

## Platform-wide requirements

- One account, roles and optional school/company/team workspaces.
- Permissioned home, unified search, notification centre with quiet hours and anti-spam, user-controlled portfolio/activity and ledger.
- Secure sessions, optional MFA, session management, least-privilege RBAC and tenant isolation.
- Server-side validation and authorization, rate limiting, safe uploads, malware checks where available, safe isolated code execution, privacy/retention/deletion policies.
- Secrets management, versioned migrations, backups, monitoring, structured logs, metrics, health checks, staging, automated tests and rollback.
- AI output labelled, grounded, reviewable, metered and protected from prompt injection, data exfiltration and cross-user leakage.
- Android/mobile-first, low-data, resilient autosave/draft recovery, Naira defaults, Nigerian phone formats, accessible UI and intermittent-connectivity support.
- Admin operations for users, organisations, plans, entitlements, moderation, payments, reconciliation, AI budgets, service health, abuse signals and audit logs.

## Release waves from the brief

1. **Foundation:** repository audit, backups, protected boundaries, auth/roles, design tokens for new modules, logging, payment architecture and deployment pipeline.
2. **Wave 1:** ScamShield, FixIt, Opportunity Radar, Portfolio, Business Launchpad MVP and TECHNOVA One shell.
3. **Wave 2:** Build-to-Launch, TechLabs, Arena and Community Projects.
4. **Wave 3:** Freelance Exchange, MicroWorks, Digital Resource Library and trust/milestone workflows.
5. **Wave 4:** QuizVerse/EduVerse expansion, Campus, Cyber Range and Skill-to-Income.
6. **Expansion:** Website Guardian, Pulse, TrustCheck, PriceWatch, NetPulse, PowerPulse, API Hub, Nexora, Web3 Forge and remaining services.

## Release acceptance

A rendered page is not a shipped feature. Every release needs a complete real user journey, validated input, persistence, permission checks, meaningful loading/empty/error/success states, monitoring, support documentation, tests, mobile QA and rollback. Do not use fake AI, fabricated opportunities, fake verification, fake payment status or decorative-only dashboards.
